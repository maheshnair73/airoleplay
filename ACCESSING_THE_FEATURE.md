# Accessing the Roleplay Knowledge Hub

## Navigation Path

The **Roleplay Knowledge Hub** (Practice Materials) can be accessed from the main sidebar navigation:

### Location in Sidebar:
```
📁 Knowledge Hub
   └─ 🧠 Practice Materials  <-- Click here
   └─ 📦 My Products
   └─ 🛡️ Competitor Intel
   └─ ☑️ Knowledge Review Queue
```

## Quick Access URL
Direct URL: `/RoleplayKnowledgeHub`

## Features Available:

### For All Users:
1. **View Training Materials** - Browse all uploaded knowledge materials
2. **Practice with AI** - Start AI roleplay sessions based on specific materials
3. **Track Progress** - See your mastery level and accuracy for each material
4. **Filter & Search** - Find materials by category, type, or keywords

### For Admins/Managers:
5. **Upload Materials** - Add new training content (PDFs, videos, audio, text)
6. **Manage Materials** - Edit or delete existing materials
7. **Categorize Content** - Organize by Product Knowledge, Sales Methodology, etc.

## How It Works:

1. **Upload Training Content** (Admin)
   - Click "Add Material" button
   - Choose material type (Document, Video, Audio, or Text)
   - Set category and add description
   - Upload or paste content

2. **Practice with AI Roleplay** (All Users)
   - Browse available materials
   - Click "Practice with AI" on any material
   - AI will quiz you on the content during roleplay
   - Get real-time feedback on your knowledge retention

3. **Track Your Progress**
   - View times practiced
   - See your accuracy percentage
   - Monitor mastery level
   - Review question history

## Material Types Supported:

- **📄 Documents** - PDF files (product guides, sales playbooks, etc.)
- **🎥 Videos** - Training videos, product demos
- **🎧 Audio** - Audio training, call recordings
- **📝 Text** - Written content, scripts, talking points

## Example Use Cases:

1. **Product Training** - Upload product documentation, practice explaining features
2. **Sales Methodology** - Upload MEDDIC/SPIN guides, practice discovery questions
3. **Objection Handling** - Upload objection scripts, practice responses
4. **Compliance Training** - Upload compliance docs, test knowledge retention
5. **Competitive Intelligence** - Upload competitor analysis, practice competitive positioning
