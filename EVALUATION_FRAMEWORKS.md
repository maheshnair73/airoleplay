# Sales Evaluation Frameworks

This document outlines the different sales evaluation frameworks available in the system, their categories, and what criteria they measure.

## Available Frameworks

### 1. Cold Call Framework
**Best for:** Initial outreach, prospecting calls, cold calling practice

**Categories:**
- **Opener** (2 criteria)
  - Permission based opener?
  - Used research on prospect?

- **Social Proof** (2 criteria)
  - Provided social proof?
  - Asked if social proof was relevant?

- **Discovery** (1 criterion)
  - SDR asked for preconceptions of product?

- **Takeaway** (2 criteria)
  - Re-confirmed that the time works for the prospect?
  - Asked for success criteria for next call?

- **Closing** (2 criteria)
  - Next steps agreed upon?
  - Follow-up meeting booked?

**Total Criteria:** 9

---

### 2. MEDDIC
**Best for:** Enterprise B2B sales, complex sales cycles, qualifying opportunities

**Categories:**
- **Metrics** (3 criteria)
  - Identified quantifiable business metrics?
  - Discussed ROI or cost savings?
  - Established measurable success criteria?

- **Economic Buyer** (3 criteria)
  - Identified the economic buyer?
  - Understood budget authority?
  - Confirmed ability to make financial decisions?

- **Decision Criteria** (3 criteria)
  - Uncovered evaluation criteria?
  - Understood what matters most in selection?
  - Identified competing priorities?

- **Decision Process** (3 criteria)
  - Mapped out the buying process?
  - Identified timeline and steps?
  - Understood stakeholders involved?

- **Identify Pain** (3 criteria)
  - Discovered compelling pain points?
  - Quantified impact of current situation?
  - Created urgency to change?

- **Champion** (3 criteria)
  - Identified internal champion?
  - Built relationship with advocate?
  - Confirmed champion's influence?

**Total Criteria:** 18

---

### 3. MEDDPICC
**Best for:** Large enterprise deals, complex procurement processes, competitive situations

**Categories:** All MEDDIC categories PLUS:

- **Paper Process** (3 criteria)
  - Understood contracting/legal process?
  - Identified approval requirements?
  - Discussed procurement procedures?

- **Competition** (3 criteria)
  - Identified competitive alternatives?
  - Understood competitive landscape?
  - Positioned against competition?

**Total Criteria:** 24

---

### 4. SPIN Selling
**Best for:** Consultative selling, needs-based selling, building value through questions

**Categories:**
- **Situation Questions** (3 criteria)
  - Asked about current situation/context?
  - Gathered background information?
  - Understood existing processes?

- **Problem Questions** (3 criteria)
  - Identified specific problems/challenges?
  - Explored pain points in depth?
  - Got prospect to articulate difficulties?

- **Implication Questions** (3 criteria)
  - Explored consequences of the problem?
  - Discussed impact on business/team?
  - Built urgency around solving the issue?

- **Need-Payoff Questions** (3 criteria)
  - Asked about value of solving the problem?
  - Got prospect to describe ideal outcome?
  - Linked solution to business impact?

**Total Criteria:** 12

---

### 5. BANT
**Best for:** Quick qualification, lead scoring, determining sales-ready leads

**Categories:**
- **Budget** (3 criteria)
  - Discussed available budget?
  - Understood financial constraints?
  - Confirmed funding allocation?

- **Authority** (3 criteria)
  - Identified decision maker(s)?
  - Understood approval process?
  - Confirmed stakeholder involvement?

- **Need** (3 criteria)
  - Identified business need?
  - Understood pain points?
  - Confirmed problem severity?

- **Timeline** (3 criteria)
  - Established buying timeline?
  - Identified key dates/milestones?
  - Understood urgency?

**Total Criteria:** 12

---

### 6. CHAMP
**Best for:** Challenge-first selling, value-based selling, modern sales approach

**Categories:**
- **Challenges** (3 criteria)
  - Identified key challenges?
  - Explored impact of problems?
  - Understood business implications?

- **Authority** (3 criteria)
  - Identified decision maker(s)?
  - Understood approval process?
  - Mapped stakeholder influence?

- **Money** (3 criteria)
  - Discussed budget availability?
  - Understood ROI expectations?
  - Confirmed financial viability?

- **Prioritization** (3 criteria)
  - Understood priority level?
  - Identified competing initiatives?
  - Confirmed timeline urgency?

**Total Criteria:** 12

## Suggested Additions/Removals

### Criteria Worth Adding:

1. **Active Listening Skills** (across all frameworks)
   - Paraphrased prospect's concerns?
   - Asked clarifying questions?
   - Avoided interrupting?

2. **Objection Handling** (especially for Cold Call & CHAMP)
   - Acknowledged objections without defensiveness?
   - Used appropriate framework (Feel-Felt-Found, etc.)?
   - Resolved concerns before closing?

3. **Value Communication** (MEDDIC, MEDDPICC, SPIN)
   - Articulated unique value proposition?
   - Tailored pitch to prospect's specific needs?
   - Differentiated from status quo?

4. **Call Control** (Cold Call Framework)
   - Maintained conversation flow?
   - Redirected when off-track?
   - Managed time effectively?

5. **Relationship Building** (all frameworks)
   - Built genuine rapport?
   - Showed empathy and understanding?
   - Established credibility?

### Criteria Worth Removing/Consolidating:

1. **"Asked if social proof was relevant?"** (Cold Call Framework)
   - Could be consolidated into "Provided social proof?" as a single criterion
   - Validation can be implied in how social proof is used

2. **"Confirmed champion's influence?"** (MEDDIC/MEDDPICC)
   - Overlaps significantly with "Built relationship with advocate"
   - Could be a sub-point rather than separate criterion

### Enhanced Generic Scorecard (Recommended)

A universal scorecard that works across all call types:

**Categories:**
1. **Opening & Rapport** (3 criteria)
   - Permission-based opening
   - Used pre-call research
   - Built initial rapport

2. **Discovery & Qualification** (4 criteria)
   - Identified business challenges/needs
   - Quantified impact with metrics
   - Understood decision-making process
   - Identified budget/authority

3. **Value Articulation** (3 criteria)
   - Provided relevant social proof
   - Tailored solution to needs
   - Differentiated from alternatives

4. **Objection Handling** (2 criteria)
   - Acknowledged concerns appropriately
   - Resolved objections effectively

5. **Closing & Next Steps** (3 criteria)
   - Created urgency to move forward
   - Agreed on clear next steps
   - Confirmed stakeholder involvement

**Total: 15 criteria** - Balanced across all call types while maintaining specificity.

## Framework Selection Guide

**Choose Cold Call Framework when:**
- Making initial outreach calls
- Practicing prospecting techniques
- Training new SDRs

**Choose MEDDIC when:**
- Selling enterprise solutions ($50K+)
- Complex B2B sales cycles
- Need rigorous qualification

**Choose MEDDPICC when:**
- Very large deals ($500K+)
- Multiple stakeholders and departments
- Formal procurement processes

**Choose SPIN when:**
- Consultative selling approach
- Building value through discovery
- Solution selling methodology

**Choose BANT when:**
- Quick lead qualification needed
- High volume sales environment
- Inside sales/transactional sales

**Choose CHAMP when:**
- Value-based selling
- Modern sales approach (challenges before budget)
- When buyer has multiple options

**Choose Generic Scorecard when:**
- General sales skills assessment
- Mixed call types in training
- Comprehensive evaluation needed
