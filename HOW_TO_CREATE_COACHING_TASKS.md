# How to Create Coaching Tasks with Training Materials

## Where to Find This Feature

### Step-by-Step Navigation

**1. Log in to the Application**
- Use your manager or admin credentials
- This feature is available to managers, admins, and company admins

**2. Navigate to Coaching Hub**
- Look at the **left sidebar navigation**
- Find the section called **"AI Sales Coach"**
- Click on **"Coaching Hub"** (has a graduation cap icon 🎓)

**3. Create New Task**
- On the Coaching Hub page, look for the **"+ Create Task"** button
- It's typically located in the top-right corner of the page
- Click this button to start creating a new coaching task

## The 4-Tab Creation Process

Once you click "Create Task", you'll see **4 tabs** at the top:

### Tab 1: Task Details ✏️

This is where you define what the task is about.

**Required Fields:**
- **Task Title**: Give your task a clear name
  - Example: "Product Demo Practice - Q1 2024"
  - Example: "Objection Handling - Pricing"

- **Scenario Description**: Describe what the sales rep should do
  - Example: "Deliver a 5-minute product demo focusing on our new analytics dashboard. Handle at least one technical objection."

**Optional Fields:**
- **Task Type**: Choose how reps will submit
  - 🎤 Audio Recording (voice only)
  - 🎥 Video Recording (with camera)
  - 🖥️ Screen Recording (screen + voice)

- **Language**: Select the language for the task
  - English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese

- **Duration**: Set time limit in minutes (3-10 recommended)
  - Example: 5 minutes for a pitch, 10 minutes for a full demo

- **Due Date**: Optional deadline for completion

**When finished with Tab 1:**
- Click **"Next: Training Materials"** button at the bottom

---

### Tab 2: Training Materials 📚 (NEW!)

This is the **new feature** that lets you assign reading materials and AI practice.

#### Section A: AI Roleplay Setup (Optional)

**What it does:**
- After completing the task, reps can practice with an AI client
- The AI will ask questions based on the training materials
- Think of it as a practice conversation

**How to use it:**
1. Click the **"Select AI Client"** dropdown
2. Choose an AI client that matches your scenario:
   - Example: "Sarah Johnson - VP of Sales at TechCorp" for a sales pitch
   - Example: "Mike Chen - CTO at Enterprise Corp" for technical demos
3. Or leave it blank if no AI practice is needed

#### Section B: Training Materials

**What it does:**
- Assigns reading materials for reps to study
- Can require them to read before submitting
- Materials are used by AI during roleplay

**The Important Toggle:**

```
┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│ Require Material Review Before Starting    [ON/OFF] │
│ When enabled, sales reps must read all selected     │
│ materials before they can start the roleplay or     │
│ submit their task                                    │
└─────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

- **Toggle ON** = Reps MUST read all materials first (recommended for new topics)
- **Toggle OFF** = Materials available for reference only (good for experienced reps)

**Selecting Materials:**

Below the toggle, you'll see a list of all available training materials with checkboxes:

```
☐ Product Features Guide
  Category: Product Knowledge | Type: Document
  Complete overview of all product features and capabilities

☐ Objection Handling Playbook
  Category: Sales Methodology | Type: Document
  Framework for handling common sales objections

☐ Pricing and ROI Calculator
  Category: Product Knowledge | Type: Spreadsheet
  Tool for calculating customer ROI and pricing scenarios
```

**How to select materials:**
1. Check the box next to each material you want to assign
2. You can select multiple materials
3. They'll be presented to reps in the order you select them
4. If no materials exist, you'll see a button to go to the Knowledge Hub

**What reps will see:**
- A "Training Materials" tab when viewing the task
- All selected materials with descriptions
- If required, they can't proceed until reading all materials
- During AI roleplay, the AI will reference these materials

**When finished with Tab 2:**
- Click **"Next: Assignment"** button at the bottom

---

### Tab 3: Assignment 👥

This is where you choose who should complete the task.

**Options:**
1. **Assign to specific team members:**
   - Click the dropdown "Select Users to Assign"
   - Choose individual team members
   - You'll see badges showing who's assigned
   - Click the × to remove someone

2. **Assign to everyone:**
   - Leave the selection empty
   - The task will be available to all team members

**When finished with Tab 3:**
- Click **"Next: Evaluation"** button at the bottom

---

### Tab 4: Evaluation ⚙️

This is where you define how you'll grade submissions.

**Default Criteria** (you can modify these):
- **Clarity**: How clear and concise was the message?
- **Confidence**: How confident and persuasive was the delivery?
- **Value Proposition**: How well was the value proposition articulated?

**To add custom criteria:**
1. Click **"Add Criterion"**
2. Enter a name (e.g., "Technical Accuracy")
3. Enter a description (e.g., "Did they explain the technical features correctly?")
4. Add as many as needed

**To remove a criterion:**
- Click the X next to any criterion you don't need

**When finished with Tab 4:**
- Click **"Create Task"** button at the bottom
- Your task is now live!

## What Happens After You Create the Task

### For You (Manager):
1. Task appears in your Coaching Hub
2. You can see which reps have started/completed it
3. You can review their submissions
4. You can see their AI roleplay performance (if you assigned an AI client)

### For Sales Reps:
1. Task appears in their Coaching Hub
2. They click to open the task
3. They see **3 tabs**:
   - **Submissions & Progress**: Their status
   - **Task Details**: Instructions and scenario
   - **Training Materials**: The materials you assigned (if any)

4. **If materials are required:**
   - They MUST read all materials first
   - System tracks what they've read
   - They cannot submit until all materials are completed

5. **They complete the task:**
   - Record audio/video/screen based on task type
   - Follow the scenario instructions
   - Submit for your review

6. **If you assigned an AI client:**
   - After submitting, they're prompted to practice with the AI
   - AI asks questions based on the training materials
   - They get immediate feedback
   - This helps them improve before the real call

7. **You review and provide feedback:**
   - You see their submission
   - You see their AI practice results (if applicable)
   - You provide specific coaching feedback

## Complete Example Walkthrough

### Example: "New Product Feature Training"

Let's say your company just launched a new analytics dashboard feature, and you want to train your team.

#### Step 1: Navigate
- Go to **sidebar → AI Sales Coach → Coaching Hub**
- Click **"+ Create Task"**

#### Step 2: Task Details (Tab 1)
- **Task Title**: "Analytics Dashboard Demo - Q2 Launch"
- **Task Type**: Video Recording
- **Language**: English
- **Duration**: 7 minutes
- **Scenario**: "You're meeting with a prospect who needs better reporting. Demonstrate our new analytics dashboard, focusing on custom reports and real-time data. Handle at least one objection about complexity or learning curve."
- **Due Date**: 2 weeks from today
- Click **"Next: Training Materials"**

#### Step 3: Training Materials (Tab 2)

**AI Roleplay Setup:**
- Select AI Client: "Jessica Park - Head of Operations at DataFlow Inc"
- Why this persona? She's operations-focused and will ask practical questions about reports

**Training Materials:**
- Toggle **"Require Material Review"** to **ON** (it's new material)
- Check these materials:
  - ☑ "Analytics Dashboard Feature Guide" (Product Knowledge)
  - ☑ "Dashboard Use Cases & ROI Examples" (Sales Collateral)
  - ☑ "Competitor Comparison - Reporting Features" (Competitive Intelligence)

You selected 3 materials, and reps must read them before starting.

Click **"Next: Assignment"**

#### Step 4: Assignment (Tab 3)
- **Option 1**: Assign to specific reps who handle enterprise deals
- **Option 2**: Leave empty to train the whole team

For this example, let's train everyone.
- Leave the selection empty

Click **"Next: Evaluation"**

#### Step 5: Evaluation (Tab 4)
Keep the default criteria and add a custom one:
- ✓ Clarity (default)
- ✓ Confidence (default)
- ✓ Value Proposition (default)
- ➕ Add: "Feature Accuracy" - "Did they correctly explain dashboard features and capabilities?"
- ➕ Add: "Objection Handling" - "How well did they address concerns about complexity?"

Click **"Create Task"**

#### Step 6: What Your Team Experiences

**Sarah (Sales Rep) logs in:**
1. Sees notification: "New Task: Analytics Dashboard Demo"
2. Opens the task
3. Sees 3 tabs, clicks **"Training Materials"**
4. Sees message: "⚠️ You must review all training materials before starting this task"
5. Sees the 3 materials you assigned:
   - Analytics Dashboard Feature Guide
   - Dashboard Use Cases & ROI Examples
   - Competitor Comparison - Reporting Features
6. Reads all three documents (system tracks this)
7. Returns to task, records her 7-minute demo video
8. Submits for review
9. Gets prompt: "Practice with Jessica Park (AI)"
10. Practices with AI Jessica, who asks:
    - "How does this compare to Tableau?"
    - "How long does it take to set up custom reports?"
    - "Can I export data to Excel?"
11. AI gives immediate feedback on her responses
12. Sarah now feels confident for real customer calls

**You (Manager) review:**
1. Watch Sarah's demo video
2. Check her AI practice score (she got 8.5/10)
3. See she referenced the materials correctly
4. Provide feedback: "Great job on objection handling! Next time, lead with a customer success story from the use cases doc."

## Quick Reference

### Where is it?
**Sidebar → AI Sales Coach → Coaching Hub → + Create Task**

### What are the 4 tabs?
1. **Task Details** - What is the task?
2. **Training Materials** - What should they study? (NEW!)
3. **Assignment** - Who should do it?
4. **Evaluation** - How will you grade it?

### When should I require material review?
- ✅ New product launches
- ✅ New methodology or process
- ✅ Training new hires
- ✅ Complex technical topics
- ❌ Quick practice exercises
- ❌ Experienced reps who know the topic

### When should I assign an AI client?
- ✅ Always! It's free practice
- ✅ Especially for challenging personas (skeptical CFO, technical CTO)
- ✅ When reps need to test their knowledge
- ❌ Skip it only if the task is purely about recording quality (like pitch video review)

## Troubleshooting

### "I don't see the Coaching Hub in my navigation"
**Solution**: Check your role. This feature is for:
- Managers
- Company Admins
- Super Admins

If you're a sales rep, you'll see Coaching Hub but can only view tasks, not create them.

### "I don't see any training materials to select"
**Solution**:
1. You need to upload materials first
2. Click the **"Go to Knowledge Hub"** button (appears when no materials exist)
3. Or go to **Sidebar → Knowledge Hub → Practice Materials**
4. Upload documents, then return to create your task

### "The + Create Task button doesn't work"
**Solution**:
1. Make sure you're logged in as a manager or admin
2. Try refreshing the page
3. Check your browser console for errors

### "Reps say they can't see the training materials"
**Solution**:
1. Open the task and go to the Training Materials tab
2. Verify materials are actually selected
3. Check that the materials are still "active" in the Knowledge Hub

## Tips for Success

### 1. Start Small
- Create your first task with 1-2 materials
- Assign to a small group or yourself for testing
- Get comfortable with the workflow
- Then scale up

### 2. Name Materials Clearly
- Bad: "Document 1", "Sales stuff"
- Good: "Q2 Product Guide", "Pricing Objection Framework"
- Reps should know what it is from the title

### 3. Match AI Client to Scenario
- Budget discussions → CFO persona
- Technical demos → CTO persona
- General sales → VP of Sales persona
- Beginners → "Friendly" personas

### 4. Set Realistic Due Dates
- Factor in time to read materials
- Factor in time to practice
- Factor in time to record submission
- Typical: 1-2 weeks for comprehensive tasks

### 5. Review Regularly
- Don't let submissions pile up
- Provide feedback within 2-3 days
- Reference the materials in your feedback
- Suggest additional AI practice if needed
